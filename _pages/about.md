Welcome to my portfolio! I’m a BSc Mathematics graduate currently pursuing a PG Diploma in Data Science and AI at COEP Technological University. My journey has been rich and diverse, with experiences spanning projection mapping, web development, drone swarming, and VR syncing.

Since high school, I’ve been passionate about technology, working on various innovative projects. A highlight of my journey was collaborating with former ISRO scientists on cutting-edge hardware at Suresh Naik Space Park in Pune. These experiences have deepened my interest in exploring the vast potential of Data Science and AI/ML and their wide-ranging applications. I am also enthusiastic about blending technology with creativity to solve real-world problems.

This site is a space to showcase my projects in niche areas like drone swarming and projection mapping, where resources are often limited. I’m also excited to share tutorials on these topics, making them more accessible and engaging. Stay tuned!
